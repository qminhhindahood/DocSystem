# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vietnamese public-sector staff, administrative officers, and document specialists who draft, review, retrieve, and manage government documents. They work in focused, accuracy-sensitive sessions and need the interface to make complex document workflows feel dependable and easy to scan.

## Product Purpose

DocAI helps users generate Vietnamese administrative documents, apply and review DOCX templates, inspect document fidelity, manage a document library, and ask source-grounded questions. Success means users can move from source material to a compliant, reviewable document without losing confidence in provenance, formatting, or system state.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, authoritative. The product should feel technically capable without looking experimental, institutional without feeling bureaucratic, and polished without imitating a consumer-electronics storefront.

## Anti-references

- Neon “AI dashboard” styling with glowing borders, purple gradients, floating orbs, or decorative glass panels.
- Literal Apple-site imitation that turns task screens into sparse product-marketing tiles.
- Dense enterprise software that relies on heavy borders, cramped controls, or ambiguous status colors.
- Decorative motion, novelty controls, and visual effects that compete with document content.
- Inconsistent English and Vietnamese interface copy within the same workflow.

## Design Principles

1. Let the document remain the visual center of gravity; application chrome should support review and editing rather than compete with it.
2. Use one quiet action language across every surface: Action Blue for primary actions and current selection, with semantic colors reserved for system state.
3. Earn confidence through legible hierarchy, explicit provenance, predictable controls, and complete loading, empty, error, and success states.
4. Adapt the spacious editorial qualities of `DESIGN.md` to each context: expressive on public pages, compact and familiar inside authenticated workflows.
5. Keep light and dark themes equally intentional, accessible, and structurally identical so changing theme never changes meaning or workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, skip links, semantic labels, reduced-motion behavior, and at least 44 by 44 pixel touch targets for primary mobile actions. Do not encode state by color alone. Vietnamese text and diacritics must remain legible at all supported viewport sizes, and dense document content must retain comfortable line height and contrast in both themes.
